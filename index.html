Important Rules

Furry & Commission Guidelines

Please review the subject matter permissions and reference sheet rules before submitting a request.

Subject Matter Rules

What I WILL Draw (SFW Tiers)

  • Furry & Anthro characters (Canines, Felines, Dragons, Scalies, Birds)
  • Feral animals, mythical beasts, & fantasy monsters
  • Detailed landscapes, floral accents, and atmospheric lighting
  • Fantasy armor, weapons, & mech accent pieces

What I WON'T Draw (NSFW Restrictions)

  • Explicit NSFW / Sexual content / Hardcore fetish art
  • Hate speech, discriminatory imagery, or offensive iconography
  • Copying another artist's artwork or style verbatim

Reference & Rights Guidelines

Reference Sheet Requirements

Please provide clear digital reference sheets with unshaded color swatches. If you do not have a full reference sheet, written descriptions with moodboard image links are acceptable for an added custom character design fee (+$30).

Personal vs Commercial Rights

  • Personal Use: Avatars, phone wallpapers, physical printing for personal display, social media headers.
  • Commercial Use: VTuber assets, merchandise resale, album artwork, brand logos, promotional flyers.
Legal Protection

Terms of Service & Business Policies

Clear contractual terms protecting both client rights and studio operations.

A non-refundable 50% deposit is required before work commences on any commission. The remaining 50% balance is due upon approval of the watermarked final preview before high-resolution or physical shipping takes place. Invoices are issued securely via Gumroad or Stripe.
Commissions include 2 major revision passes during the rough sketch stage. Major lineart or pose adjustments requested after sketch approval will incur additional fees. Refunds are offered at 100% minus processing fees if cancelled before sketch phase, 50% during sketch phase, and 0% after lineart completion.
Physical original canvas paintings and stickers are packaged in reinforced moisture-resistant bubble mailers. Waterproof vinyl stickers require 24 hours post-application before exposure to heavy moisture. Tracking numbers are emailed upon dispatch.
